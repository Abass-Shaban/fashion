Frequently Asked Questions

🌟 What sizes do you offer?

We offer a wide range of sizes from XS to XXL. A detailed size chart is available on each product page to help you find the perfect fit.

🥻 What materials do you use?

We use premium quality fabrics including 100% cotton, polyester blends, and sustainable materials. Each product description specifies the exact materials used.

💫 Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. We accept returns or exchanges within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, unwashed, and in its original condition with tags attached.

🕐 How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within 2–5 business days after confirmation, depending on your location within Kenya.

🤔 How do I know which size will fit me best?

Please refer to our size guide and compare it with your body measurements. If you're unsure, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

📦 Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within Kenya only. We're working on expanding to international shipping soon.
